🕹️ HOW TO PLAY & GUIDE

🔴
1. Power On & Start: Tap the highlighted RED ON/OFF button to power on and start playing.
🎮
2. Switch Games (4-in-1 Suite): Tap the bottom [GAME: MODE A/B/C/D 🔄] button to easily toggle between:
  • Mode A: Classic Tetris (Lines + Hold Queue)
  • Mode B: Tank Battle (Shoot enemy tanks)
  • Mode C: Formula Racing (Dodge traffic + Turbo)
  • Mode D: Retro Snake (Eat dots, grow tail)
⚡
3. Speed & Difficulty (1 to 20): Tap the ◀ SPD ▶ buttons below the screen to change game speed from relaxed (01) to master (20).
⌨️
4. Controls: Desktop PC: Arrow Keys or WASD to steer, Space / W to shoot / rotate, C to hold piece.
Mobile Touch: Left thumb steers, Right thumb rotates & fires.
